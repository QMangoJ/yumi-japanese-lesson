01 — ABOUT ME

About Me

Hi there! I'm Yumi, a certified Japanese teacher living in Chiba Prefecture, originally from Aomori in Japan's Tohoku region. People say I'm bright and friendly — and I love traveling abroad.

I know how hard learning a language can be, because I struggled with English myself at first. That experience became my strength: 13 years of teaching English to beginners taught me how to support learners who are starting a foreign language for the very first time.

I've lived in a homestay in the U.S. and studied abroad in Australia for a year, and backpacked around Southeast Asia as a student. In Cambodia, when everything I had was stolen, travelers and locals helped me — and I've wanted to give back to people traveling to Japan ever since.

07 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it okay if I am a complete beginner?

Absolutely! Everyone is more than welcome. Let's start with a relaxed feeling, just like catching up with a friend — we'll begin with hiragana and katakana and build up with the GENKI textbook.

Can I learn specific phrases or Japanese from my favorite anime?

Yes! I will fully customize the material so you can learn the exact, real-life Japanese you want to use right now.

How do we schedule our lesson times?

Book any open slot on this page at least 72 hours in advance. Students using lesson packages can freely book and adjust their lesson duration from 30 minutes up to 2 hours to fit their weekly schedule — just mention it in the booking notes.

Am I allowed to ask questions in English?

Yes, definitely! I will use English to support you and make sure you gain a crystal-clear, deep understanding of the language.

When can I cancel or reschedule a lesson?

You can cancel a confirmed lesson from My Bookings at any time, and reschedule it up to 24 hours before it starts, with up to two reschedules per booking. You get a refund when you have not rescheduled the lesson and you cancel at least 24 hours before it starts — the amount paid goes back to your original payment method. If you cancel within 24 hours, or cancel a lesson you have already rescheduled, the lesson is still cancelled but it counts as used and nothing is returned. New bookings and new rescheduled times must be selected at least 72 hours in advance.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payments are processed securely through Stripe. You can use major credit or debit cards available at checkout, including Visa, Mastercard, and American Express.

What tool do you use for lessons? Do I need to prepare anything in advance?

Lessons are held on Google Meet. You do not need to install a special app — simply click the link in your booking confirmation to join from your browser.

Do I need to bring or prepare anything before my lesson?

No special textbook or materials are required. If you have topics you would like to discuss or Japanese phrases you have wondered about in daily life or while traveling, making a quick note beforehand will help us get started smoothly.
