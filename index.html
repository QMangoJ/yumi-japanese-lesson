01 — ABOUT ME

About Me

Hi there! I'm Yumi, a certified Japanese teacher living in Chiba Prefecture, originally from Aomori in Japan's Tohoku region. People say I'm bright and friendly — and I love traveling abroad.

I know how hard learning a language can be, because I struggled with English myself at first. That experience became my strength: 13 years of teaching English to beginners taught me how to support learners who are starting a foreign language for the very first time.

I've lived in a homestay in the U.S. and studied abroad in Australia for a year, and backpacked around Southeast Asia as a student. In Cambodia, when everything I had was stolen, travelers and locals helped me — and I've wanted to give back to people traveling to Japan ever since.

My lessons are fun and relaxed, like hanging out with a friend. Tell me your goals and interests, and we'll find your path together.

05 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it okay if I am a complete beginner?

Absolutely! Beginners are my specialty. We'll start with hiragana and katakana and build up step by step with the GENKI textbook — at your pace, with English support whenever you need it.

Can you support me in English?

Yes. I speak English (and some Spanish, Khmer, and French), and I spent 13 years teaching English, so explaining Japanese in English is something I do every day.

What kind of lessons can I take?

Conversation practice, beginner foundations, JLPT N5–N4 preparation, travel Japanese, and business Japanese — every plan is customized to your goals. See the lesson menu.

How do I book, reschedule, or cancel?

All booking is handled through Preply: pick a time that suits you on my Preply profile, and reschedule or cancel from your account there according to Preply's policy.

Do I need to prepare anything before my lesson?

Nothing special. If there are topics you'd like to talk about or phrases you've wondered about, jotting them down beforehand helps us get started smoothly.
