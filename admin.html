Schedule

Lessons already booked this month. Click any day to see its lessons and teacher remarks; to change availability, go to Availability.

Weekly hours (Japan time)

Click a cell to toggle whether that hour is bookable (pink = bookable, grey = not); drag with the mouse to paint several cells, or tap one by one on a phone. The "All day" button at the top of each column selects/clears the whole day; the small text under each column summarises the day. Lessons start on the hour and must fit entirely inside the selected hours. Individual dates can be adjusted in the availability calendar below.

Availability calendar

Click any day to adjust it individually: a day off, special hours, or blocking a few start times. What the calendar shows is exactly what students can book.

Blocked dates

Holidays and days off — no lessons can be booked on these dates. One date per line, as YYYY-MM-DD. (The calendar above can do the same per day.)

Prices (USD)

The per-lesson price for each duration (USD). Saving applies immediately to new bookings and new packages; existing bookings and packages keep the price they were bought at.

Email (Resend)

Mail is sent as “Yumi Japanese Lessons <yumi-lesson@ki-toko.com>” via Resend.

Booking emails are always recorded in the outbox. They are actually sent only when both the From address and a Resend API key are set.

Meeting & Calendar

Shown in every booking confirmation and email. Leave empty and students will see “your teacher will send the meeting link before the lesson”.

Lessons feed (ICS) — give this URL to Yumi once; she adds it in Google Calendar under Settings → Add calendar → From URL, and every paid lesson then appears in her calendar automatically (cancellations drop out on their own). Anyone with the URL can see booking names — keep it private, and regenerate the key to revoke access.

Access · Admin allowlist

Student accounts registered/signed in with these emails see an "Admin" entry in the top-right corner (just a shortcut — entering the admin still requires admin sign-in). One email per line; saving takes effect immediately.

Google sign-in

Let students sign in with one click using their Google account (accounts are merged by email). Create an OAuth 2.0 client (Web application) in Google Cloud Console, register the redirect URI below, then fill in the Client ID and Client Secret here. The Google button appears in the sign-in dialog only once both are set.

Subscriptions · Packages

Student lesson packages (6 / 10 / 20 lessons, 1–3 fixed weekly times). This page can only stop a package — stopping cancels all remaining lessons and refunds them by the usual rules (no refund inside 12 hours of a lesson); lessons already taken are unaffected.

Details

How it is counted: single-lesson revenue is attributed to the lesson date (JST) at the amount recorded when booked; cancelled-and-fully-refunded bookings are excluded; lessons cancelled inside 12 hours without a refund still count (the money was kept). Package revenue is attributed to the purchase (payment) date at the full package price, regardless of lesson dates. Lesson counts include package lessons. Weeks start on Sunday (same as the admin calendars).
